## Editorial method

- Pascha dates for 2026–2030 were checked against the Orthodox Church in America’s published Paschal-cycle table.
- Fixed commemorations are curated principal entries, not a claim to reproduce every jurisdiction’s complete synaxarion.
- Martyr profiles are brief original summaries checked against Orthodox reference material where available. Approximate dates use “c.” and sparse traditions are identified cautiously.
- Every “About the Martyr” field is limited to 149 characters.
- Historical spotlights are brief original summaries of widely documented Church history.
- Traditional core prayers are identified by title and presented in common English forms.
- Newly written devotional prayers are labeled as original in their categories or descriptions.
- No third-party website is scraped or contacted by the running app.
- No remote content is silently substituted for the packaged local data.

Calendar use should remain connected to a real Orthodox parish and its appointed clergy.
